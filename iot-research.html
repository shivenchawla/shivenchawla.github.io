Research focus / Network & IoT security

Deep learning for IoT intrusion detection

Applying machine learning to detect intrusions in connected environments.

The research question

How can machine learning support intrusion detection in IoT networks? This question shaped my graduate research at the University of Washington Bothell and subsequent publications with Geethapriya Thamilarasu.

The published work

Sensors, 2019

Towards Deep-Learning-Driven Intrusion Detection for the Internet of Things

Geethapriya Thamilarasu and Shiven Chawla

The journal paper explores deep learning for intrusion detection in IoT networks. The publisher's version provides the methodology, evaluation, and results.

Read the paper ↗

Research foundations

The work connects a master's thesis on deep-learning-based intrusion detection with a conference paper on real-time intrusion detection as a service.

  • Security as a Service: Real-time Intrusion Detection in Internet of ThingsConference paper · 2018 · Shiven Chawla and Geethapriya Thamilarasu
  • Deep Learning based Intrusion Detection System for Internet of ThingsMaster's thesis · 2017 · Shiven Chawla

A continuing interest in connected systems

This research established a foundation in connected-system security. Cyber-physical and industrial control environments are a future research interest, extending the question of adversarial behavior to settings where software and physical processes interact.
