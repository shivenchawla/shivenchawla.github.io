THE THREAD THROUGH IT ALL

Curiosity.
Engineering.
Adversarial thinking.

My work has moved from network and IoT security to cloud engineering, adversarial machine learning, and AI red teaming.

Today, I am a Principal Security Researcher at Microsoft in AI Red Team. I focus on red-teaming models and systems, with a particular interest in realizing end-to-end adversarial emulation scenarios.

Earlier, in Azure Trustworthy Machine Learning, I worked on Counterfit and contributed to MITRE ATLAS. That team later became AI Red Team. My background also includes cloud infrastructure at Microsoft, engineering at Amazon, and engineering leadership and security at GE Healthcare.

I hold an M.S. in Cyber Security Engineering from the University of Washington Bothell and a B.Tech. in Computer Science & Engineering from Sharda University.
