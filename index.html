I investigate the security of AI models and systems through red teaming, with a particular interest in realizing end-to-end adversarial emulation scenarios.

Research on machine-learning-based intrusion detection for IoT networks established a foundation in securing connected systems. That research thread extends into adversarial machine learning and generative AI security, connecting publications, practical security assessments, contributions to shared threat knowledge, and public presentations.

Experience building and securing production systems grounds this research in practice. Looking ahead, cyber-physical and industrial control environments are compelling settings for exploring how AI, software, and physical processes interact under adversarial conditions.
