Adversarial research / BSides Seattle, 2025

From weaknesses to attack scenarios

Realizing end-to-end adversarial emulation scenarios is a central focus of my work red-teaming AI models and systems.

The research perspective

Individual weaknesses are part of a larger picture. An end-to-end scenario examines how those weaknesses connect in a system, bringing the surrounding context and potential impact into the assessment.

This perspective connects model-level adversarial behavior with the security of the systems in which models operate.
