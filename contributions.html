Collaborative prototype / BlueHat 2024

PyRIT Ship

Developed with my colleague Joris, PyRIT Ship is a prototype for integrating PyRIT into existing security tooling. It exposes prompt-generation and scoring capabilities through an API, with a Burp Suite extension for use from Intruder.

We presented the project at Microsoft's BlueHat 2024 conference. The repository describes it as a prototype and passion project, not a maintained production product.
